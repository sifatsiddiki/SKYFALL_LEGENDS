About the Game

Skyfall Legends is a fast-paced vertical scrolling shooter developed as part of our Games Engineering coursework. You pilot a customizable jet through three handcrafted stages, dodging dense bullet patterns and facing an epic boss battle.

Key Features

  • Three-stage campaign with escalating difficulty and a final boss fight.
  • Wave-based enemy spawns driven by data (no hard-coded spawn logic).
  • Multiple playable jets and difficulty levels.
  • Controller and keyboard support with remappable controls.
  • Explosions, hit flashes, and reactive HUD with low-life warning.
  • Full options menu: resolution, windowed/fullscreen, audio and VSync.
